1.3 — Responsive Typography (clamp)

Heading sizes are static. A 60px heading on a 375px phone takes 16% of viewport width.
Fix: Replace static rem values with clamp(min, preferred, max). Body sizes stay static. Zero component changes.

Current (static at all viewports)

At 375px (phone):

heading-2xl (60px)

heading-xl (48px)

heading-lg (36px)

heading-md (32px)

Proposed (fluid scaling)

At 375px (phone) → scales up at wider viewports:

heading-2xl 36→60px

heading-xl 32→48px

heading-lg 28→36px

heading-md 24→32px

Resize this browser window to see the headings scale fluidly. At desktop width they match the current sizes exactly.

1.4 — Body Text Letter Spacing

All 4 body sizes use -0.02em tracking. At small sizes this hurts readability (negative tracking on 10-12px text).
Fix: Differentiate by size. Large text slightly tighter, small text slightly looser. Follows Material Design 3 pattern.

Current (all -0.02em)
body-lg The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aa Bb Cc 123
body-md The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aa Bb Cc 123
body-sm The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aa Bb Cc 123 -0.02em
body-xs The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aa Bb Cc 123 -0.02em
Proposed (graduated)
body-lg The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aa Bb Cc 123 -0.01em
body-md The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aa Bb Cc 123 0em
body-sm The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aa Bb Cc 123 +0.01em
body-xs The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Aa Bb Cc 123 +0.02em
