Killing the accent rail

The colored side-stripe on a card is, across every source, the single most recognizable AI tell. We removed it from Card in v0.44.0 — but it still ships as the default in four places. Here is the rail-free treatment for each, before we touch code.

Still shipping the rail: toast · ai/blocks (×5) · schedule-view · chat mention Method: replace, don't just delete — curated per context
◆ The rule (Card's "elevation over edge", generalized)

Carry meaning through the soft tinted surface + a typed icon or dot + a text label — never a stripe. Never color alone.

This is our own variant="soft" philosophy (step-3 bg, step-11 text) applied to status. It also satisfies the accessibility floor every source names: ≥3 non-color indicators, so it survives color-blindness and forced-colors. Precedent — Radix Callout (soft tint + icon, no rail), Carbon Notification (icon + text + subtle fill), and Discord's retreat from heavy mention tinting toward a quieter token.
