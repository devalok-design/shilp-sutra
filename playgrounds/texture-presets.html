Texture playground

Toggle [data-texture] to add surface tactility. Especially noticeable on accent and dark surfaces.

Implementation: SVG feTurbulence noise overlay via pseudo-element. Inline data URI, ~600 bytes.

Standard surface

Texture is subtle on light surfaces. Registers in peripheral vision more than direct attention — adds tactility without competing with content.

Accent surface

Texture reads more strongly against tinted backgrounds. Brand-tinted grain (Devalok preset) deepens the accent without changing its hue.

Dark surface

Texture is most expressive on dark surfaces. Grain feels like film stock or matte paint, lifts cheaper-looking flat dark UI.
