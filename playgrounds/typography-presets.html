Brand voice lives in the type scale.

Three presets, same content. The display headline grows from 28px (dense) through 40px (default) to 56px (editorial). Body shrinks for dense dashboards, grows for content sites. Leading and tracking move in sympathy — dense type sits closer, editorial breathes.

A heading at the second level

Body copy. The scale ratio drives how aggressively heading sizes climb above body. 1.15 keeps everything close; 1.333 creates dramatic contrast typical of magazine layouts. Leading on body adjusts independently — dense leans 1.4, editorial leans 1.6.
